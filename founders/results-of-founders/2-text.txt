🌱 We’re proud to share that Team FEBA won 2nd place at the Green Tech Hackathon 2025! 🥈

From March 18 to 20, over an intense and inspiring 3-day challenge, we built ZeroWaste project – an automated service that detects and shares nearby out-of-date or soon-to-expire products. Our goal was to create a solution that’s both ecologically impactful and financially efficient – and we’re proud of what we achieved.

This success wouldn’t have been possible without my incredible teammates: Diyorbek Ibragimov, Shoxjahon, Marjona and Dilshod Sultonov.  I’m truly thankful for the experience, the teamwork, and the new friends I gained during this journey. 💚
